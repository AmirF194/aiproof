About aiproof

aiproof is a structured ranking of 1,000 software, AI, security, platform, product, and engineering-leadership roles, scored on four axes (demand, automation resistance, skill depth, strategic importance) over a 2026–2035 horizon.

Each entry corresponds to a job title that posts as its own listing on Indeed, LinkedIn, or Levels.fyi today. The point is to expose where the underlying work — not the title — is durable.

Read the methodology for scoring rubric and sources, the deep insights for the data behind the headlines, and the full report for per-role analysis.
