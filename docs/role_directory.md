## Engineering Leadership (45)

- **Chief AI Officer** — C-suite owner of AI strategy, model spend, and the company-wide AI roadmap.
- **Chief Architect** — Top architecture role; owns cross-cutting technical patterns and standards.
- **Chief Data Officer (CDO)** — Top data exec; owns data strategy, governance, and the analytics org.
- **Chief Digital Officer** — Top digital-transformation exec across the business.
- **Chief Information Officer (CIO)** — Top corporate-IT exec; owns internal systems and IT strategy.
- **Chief Information Security Officer** — Top security exec; owns risk, breach response, and reporting to the board.
- **Chief Product Officer** — Top product exec; owns product strategy, vision, and the PM org.
- **CTO** — Chief technology officer; owns engineering strategy, architecture, and senior hiring.
- **Deputy CISO** — Second-in-command security exec; runs ops while CISO handles board and audits.
- **Director of AI / ML** — Senior leader of the AI/ML org.
- **Director of Data** — Senior leader of the data org.
- **Director of Design** — Senior design leader; runs design across several products.
- **Director of Engineering** — Senior eng leader; owns 30–100 engineers across several teams.
- **Director of Infrastructure** — Senior leader of the infra org.
- **Director of Platform** — Senior leader of the platform / IDP org.
- **Director of Product** — Senior product leader; runs a PM group.
- **Director of Security** — Senior leader of the security org.
- **Distinguished Engineer** — Highest-tier IC; sets multi-year technical direction across the company.
- **Engineering Chief of Staff** — Runs operations and strategy support directly for an eng exec.
- **Engineering Manager** — First-line eng leader; owns 6–10 engineers, planning, hiring, performance.
- **Engineering Operations Manager** — Runs program ops, planning, and execution support across an eng org.
- **Fellow Engineer** — Top-tier IC; equivalent to or above Distinguished.
- **Group Engineering Manager** — Manager-of-managers; owns 3–6 EMs and ~50 engineers.
- **Head of Backend** — Owns the backend org and core-services standards.
- **Head of DevOps / SRE** — Owns the DevOps / SRE / platform org.
- **Head of Engineering** — Top eng leader at the org or business unit; equivalent to VP at smaller cos.
- **Head of Frontend** — Owns the frontend org and platform-frontend standards.
- **Head of Mobile** — Owns the mobile org across iOS / Android / cross-platform teams.
- **Head of QA** — Owns QA strategy and headcount; rare at modern eng-led orgs.
- **Principal Engineer** — IC tier above Staff; owns architecture across multiple teams or products.
- **Senior Director of Engineering** — Director-level eng leader at scale; owns multiple groups.
- **Senior Engineering Manager** — Engineering manager with 5+ years of management; usually owns 1–2 teams.
- **Senior Principal Engineer** — Above-Principal IC tier; cross-org architecture and influence.
- **Senior Staff Engineer** — Above-Staff IC; ships company-defining architecture work.
- **Staff Engineer / Tech Lead** — Senior IC who owns architecture, judgment, and cross-team coordination.
- **SVP of Engineering** — Top engineering exec at scale; oversees multiple VPs and 500+ engineers.
- **Tech Lead Manager** — Hybrid IC + EM role; ~50% coding + ~50% people management.
- **VP of AI / ML** — Engineering exec for AI/ML; allocates GPU spend and model-team headcount.
- **VP of Data** — Engineering exec for the data org (warehouses, pipelines, analytics).
- **VP of Engineering** — Engineering exec; runs the eng org's hiring, planning, and execution.
- **VP of Infrastructure** — Engineering exec for infrastructure (cloud, networking, capacity).
- **VP of Platform** — Engineering exec for the platform org (developer platforms, IDPs, internal tooling).
- **VP of Product** — Top product leader; runs the PM org and partners with engineering execs.
- **VP of Product Engineering** — Engineering exec for product-shipping teams (vs platform/infra).
- **VP of Security** — Engineering exec for security; accountable for risk and incident response.
